# Discovery Basics

Discovery is how each side learns what evidence the other side has. The federal toolbox is in **Rules 26-37**.

## The four main tools

| Tool | Rule | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Initial disclosures | 26(a)(1) | Auto-exchange of basic info before anyone has to ask |
| Interrogatories | 33 | Written questions answered under oath. **Limit: 25** including subparts. |
| Requests for production | 34 | Ask for documents, ESI, things, or to enter property |
| Requests for admission | 36 | Yes/no factual statements. **No response = ADMITTED.** |
| Depositions | 30, 31 | Recorded testimony under oath |

Use Forseti's **Motion Drafts** tab for blank templates of each.

## Timing

- **Rule 26(d)**: no formal discovery until the parties have held a **Rule 26(f) conference**.
- **Rule 26(f)**: parties meet at least **21 days before** the scheduling conference and produce a discovery plan.
- **30 days** is the default response time for interrogatories, RFPs, and RFAs.
- **Rule 16** scheduling order sets the close of discovery; respect it.

## Scope (Rule 26(b)(1))

You can discover any nonprivileged matter **relevant** to a claim or defense and **proportional** to the needs of the case. Six proportionality factors:

1. importance of the issues at stake
2. amount in controversy
3. parties' relative access to information
4. parties' resources
5. importance of the discovery in resolving the issues
6. whether the burden outweighs the benefit

## Privilege

If you withhold a document on privilege grounds, you must produce a **privilege log** (Rule 26(b)(5)) that describes the document well enough for the other side to evaluate the claim - without revealing the privileged content.

## Sanctions (Rule 37)

The court can compel responses, award fees, exclude evidence, strike pleadings, or **dismiss your case** for discovery abuse. The most common pro se mistake is **ignoring** discovery requests because they look complicated. Don't. Object on the record, but respond.

## Practical workflow

1. Send your initial disclosures within **14 days** of the Rule 26(f) conference.
2. Send a single, focused first set of interrogatories and RFPs.
3. Calendar each response deadline (Forseti Deadlines tab).
4. Confer in good faith before filing any discovery motion (most local rules require it).
