# Using the Camera Capture (OCR) Workflow

Forseti can read text from a printed document or your handwriting and drop it into the blanks of a generated draft. It is a time-saver, not a substitute for proofreading.

## When to use it

- You wrote a draft answer by hand on lined paper and want to type it cleanly.
- You have a printed document with text you want to quote in your filing.
- You want to copy a court order's caption block into your own filing without typing it.

## When NOT to use it

- Anything you have to **sign**. Sign on paper.
- Court forms with handwritten boxes that have specific size requirements.
- Documents you do not have the right to copy.

## How

1. Open the **Drafts** tab and choose the draft you want to populate.
2. Tap **Capture & Fill**.
3. Hold the phone steady, frame the page so all four corners are visible, and capture.
4. The recognized text shows on the left; the draft fields show on the right. Drag a recognized block onto a field, or tap the field and tap the block.
5. Review. **Always review.** OCR mistakes happen, especially with cursive.
6. Tap **Build PDF** to render the final draft, then **Print**.

## Quality tips

- Even, diffuse lighting beats direct sun (no shadows).
- Plain background, no clutter.
- Camera parallel to the page (avoid keystone tilt).
- For handwriting: print, do not script. Use a black pen.

## Privacy

Recognition runs **on your device** with Google ML Kit. Nothing is uploaded.
