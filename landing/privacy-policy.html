Forseti is a pro se civil procedure companion for Android. This policy explains what Forseti does and does not do with your information.

1. No accounts, no backend

Forseti does not require you to create an account. We do not operate servers that receive your case data, notes, bookmarks, deadlines, drafts, scans, imported forms, uploaded rule PDFs, Contents caches, or OCR text. That information stays on your device unless you export a backup, share a file, or save a file using Android’s system sheets or file picker.

2. What is stored on your device

Forseti may store, locally only:

  • Case workspace — files and folders you add to a case profile, including Brokkr Forge ingest results and inbox triage state
  • Notes, bookmarks, and deadlines you create
  • Uploaded rules — PDFs you choose to keep on the device, plus on-device Contents and outline caches
  • Imported drafts — state or custom form PDFs you add in the Drafts tab
  • App preferences — including in-app notices accepted, first-run tour completion, light/dark appearance, and your chosen app language; these are not sent to us
  • Trial and purchase state — managed with Google Play Billing on your device (see section 6)

We do not receive copies of this data.

3. Camera and on-device OCR

Forseti uses your device’s camera only when you open the document scanner or related capture flows. Images and PDF pages may be processed on your device with Google ML Kit text recognition for scanner capture, Brokkr Forge sorting, Notes, and Contents headings for uploaded rules. Forseti does not upload camera images, recognized text, or OCR results to Asvaettir Labs.

4. Notifications

Forseti schedules local notifications for deadlines and background Brokkr Forge ingest progress or completion. We are not notified when these notices fire or what they contain.

5. Network use

Forseti uses the internet only for purposes you initiate or that are clearly public, for example:

  • Downloading official, public rules and forms from government or court sources
  • Opening reference links in your browser, including court, National Center for State Courts, and legal-aid resources listed in the app
  • Google Play for trial status, purchases, and updates

When you open an external link, your browser and that site’s policies apply. Forseti does not pass your case files or personal notes to those sites.

We do not collect analytics, advertising identifiers, or usage telemetry for Asvaettir Labs. The app does not use the advertising ID (AD_ID).

6. Trial and purchase

Forseti may offer a time-limited free trial and an optional one-time unlock through Google Play Billing. Payment handling is performed by Google. Forseti does not see or store your payment card or bank details.

During the trial, core app features remain available; some Guides are purchase-gated. Purchased users may change app language and light/dark appearance. Those preferences remain on the device.

7. App language and localization

At first launch you choose a display language (English, Spanish, or Simplified Chinese) for menus, guides, and in-app help. That choice is stored on your device and is not transmitted to us. Official court PDFs and bundled federal forms remain in English regardless of UI language.

Translations are not certified and may be inaccurate. English rules control. You are solely responsible for verifying information before you file. See our Terms of Use.

8. Backups, share, and save to device

If you use backup, Forseti writes an archive to a location you choose on your device or cloud storage you control. Print, Share, and Save-to-device actions likewise use Android system sheets or the Storage Access Framework under your control. We do not receive backup files or shared copies.

9. Children

Forseti is intended for adult self-represented litigants and is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country).

10. Terms of Use

Use of Forseti is also governed by our Terms of Use, including disclaimers of legal advice, translation accuracy, and limitation of liability.

11. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. We will change the effective date at the top when we do.

12. Contact

Forseti is information only — not legal advice. You are responsible for your filings and legal decisions.
