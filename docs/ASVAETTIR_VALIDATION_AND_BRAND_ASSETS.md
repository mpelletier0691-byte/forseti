# Asvaettir Labs Validation and Brand Assets Guide

This guide defines how to validate Asvaettir Labs public claims, Wix site
content, app launch readiness, product screenshots, and brand imagery before
publication. Use it with `ASVAETTIR_WIX_ECOSYSTEM.md` and
`FORSETI_LAUNCH_FINALIZATION.md`.


## Public product scope gate

Only publish products that are built or explicitly approved for public preview.
As of the current Wix finalization pass, do not publish VoltHalla because it has
not been successfully built. Do not add a VoltHalla card, roadmap item, feature
claim, screenshot, mockup, or CTA to the public Wix page.

Current allowed public scope:

- Forseti
- Brokkr Forge
- MuninnShade, clearly framed as still in development where appropriate
- Bifrost, only with existing validated public copy or Copy Pending language
- Odin's Tower, as a built workstation / infrastructure showcase using the validated public description

If a product exists but its public claims are not yet validated, use Copy Pending
or Coming Soon rather than inventing features.

## 1. Validation principle

Every public statement should be either:

1. **Verified:** Supported by the current app, source code, store listing,
   screenshot, policy, or public product behavior.
2. **Clearly framed:** Marked as coming soon, in development, design phase,
   internal planning, or scenario modeling.
3. **Removed or softened:** Rewritten if it implies a legal outcome, guaranteed
   growth, exclusive market dominance, or a capability the product does not yet
   provide.

This protects the brand while keeping the site confident, professional, and
trustworthy.

## 2. Wix site validation checklist

Before publishing the Wix site, verify:

- All placeholder text is replaced or intentionally labeled **Coming Soon**.
- Each product has a status badge that matches the current reality.
- All buttons either link to a real destination or are labeled as early access /
  coming soon.
- Footer contains email, privacy policy, terms, and the brand tagline.
- Mobile layout is checked after each screenshot or image is added.
- No confidential audit report language, private case facts, credentials, or
  internal revenue projections appear on the public site.
- Every image has alt text.
- The site uses consistent product names:
  - Forseti
  - Brokkr Forge
  - MuninnShade
  - Bifrost
  - Odin's Tower
  - VoltHalla only if internal / hidden; do not publish
- The tone remains mythic but grounded, analytical, precise, and human-centered.

## 3. Product claim validation matrix

| Claim type | Public claim | Evidence required before publishing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pricing | Forseti is a $4.99 permanent unlock | App billing copy, Play Console product, and store listing match |
| No subscription | Forseti has no recurring subscription | Billing products contain no subscription SKU |
| Ad-free | Forseti shows no ads | No ad SDK and no ad placements in UI |
| Offline-first | Core workflows work without constant connectivity | FRCP, guides, forms, notes, deadlines, glossary, and case data work locally |
| No unnecessary data collection | App avoids analytics / tracking claims | Privacy policy and Data Safety answers match code behavior |
| Multilingual | English, Spanish, Simplified Chinese support | UI/guides/glossary translations are present and selectable |
| Official federal materials | FRCP and federal forms are from official public sources | Asset provenance is documented |
| User-selectable PDFs | Users can choose or reference PDFs | Feature exists in app or is labeled coming soon |
| Legal guidance | Procedure support only | Copy says information only, not legal advice |
| Growth projections | Install / revenue ranges | Keep internal unless explicitly labeled scenario planning |

## 6. Brand image validation

Use the following rules for Wix hero images, product images, and brand assets:

- Dark storm-gray / black base.
- Rune-gold accents.
- High contrast on mobile.
- Mythic but professional.
- No cartoon Viking styling.
- No false legal authority, government seal, court endorsement, or bar
  association implication.
- No text baked into key images unless it remains readable on mobile.
- Avoid cluttered symbols; one primary focal point is stronger.
- Preferred motifs:
  - Raven
  - Scales
  - Forge / anvil
  - Lightning sigils
  - Runic borders
  - Evidence nodes / timelines

### General Wix branding image concept

Use a square or wide hero image with:

- A raven silhouette above balanced scales of justice.
- Subtle Norse runic borders.
- Dark storm-gray background.
- Gold highlights.
- Optional blue-white lightning accents only if they support an already built
  and validated public product. Do not imply VoltHalla is available.
- No text, no people, no watermark.

Suggested alt text:

> Asvaettir Labs raven and scales brand image with gold Norse accents on a dark
> storm-gray background.

## 7. Brand consistency QA

Before publication, scan the Wix site for:

- Product names spelled consistently.
- No mixed tone between casual filler and final brand voice.
- Every product card has: tagline, status, description, features, CTA.
- Every future item says Coming Soon.
- Every app claim is either supported by product behavior or labeled in
  development.
- Founder bio and mission match the approved copy.
- Roadmap uses:
  - Forseti - Public release
  - Brokkr Forge - Beta
  - MuninnShade - v1.1 subsystem expansion
  - Bifrost - Public copy validation
  - Odin's Tower - Unified suite vision
  - VoltHalla - Internal only; do not publish
