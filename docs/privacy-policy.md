# Forseti — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** May 22, 2026  
**Last updated:** July 29, 2026  
**Publisher:** Asvaettir Labs  
**App version covered:** 0.1.19 and later (this update also describes practices in 0.1.18)

Forseti is a pro se civil procedure companion for Android. This policy explains what Forseti does and does not do with your information.

## 1. No accounts, no backend

Forseti does not require you to create an account. We do not operate servers that receive your case data, notes, bookmarks, deadlines, drafts, scans, imported forms, uploaded rule PDFs, Contents/table-of-contents caches, or OCR text. That information stays on your device unless **you** export a backup, share a file, or save a file using Android’s normal share sheet or system file picker.

## 2. What is stored on your device

Forseti may store, locally only:

- **Case workspace** — files and folders you add to a case profile, including Brokkr Forge ingest results and inbox triage state  
- **Notes, bookmarks, and deadlines** you create  
- **Uploaded rules** — PDFs you choose to keep on the device, plus on-device Contents/outline caches built from those PDFs  
- **Drafts you import** — state or custom form PDFs you add in the Drafts tab  
- **App preferences** — including whether you accepted in-app notices, completed the first-run tour, light/dark appearance preference, and your **chosen app language** (set at first launch; after purchase you may change language and appearance in Settings; not sent to us)  
- **Trial and purchase state** — managed with Google Play Billing on your device (see section 6)

We do not receive copies of this data.

## 3. Camera and on-device OCR

Forseti uses your device’s camera only when you open the document scanner or related capture flows. Images and PDF pages may be processed **on your device** with Google ML Kit text recognition to help you work with documents — for example scanner capture, Brokkr Forge sorting, Notes, and Contents headings for uploaded rules. Forseti does not upload camera images, recognized text, or OCR results to Asvaettir Labs — we do not operate servers for that purpose.

## 4. Notifications

Forseti schedules **local** notifications for deadlines you create and for background Brokkr Forge ingest progress/completion. Reminders and status notices are generated on your device. We are not notified when your reminders fire or what they contain.

## 5. Network use

Forseti uses the internet only for purposes you initiate or that are clearly public, for example:

- Downloading **official, public** rules and forms (such as federal civil procedure PDFs) from government or court sources  
- Opening **reference links** in your browser (for example Federal Judicial Center pages, National Center for State Courts education pages linked from References, court websites, legal-aid locators, and similar resources listed in the app)  
- **Google Play** for trial status, purchases, and updates  

When you open an external link, your browser and that site’s policies apply. Forseti does not pass your case files or personal notes to those sites.

We do **not** collect analytics, advertising identifiers, or usage telemetry for Asvaettir Labs. The app does not use the advertising ID (AD_ID).

## 6. Trial and purchase

Forseti may offer a time-limited free trial and an optional one-time unlock through **Google Play Billing**. Payment and receipt handling are performed by Google. Forseti does not see or store your payment card or bank details. Google’s privacy policy governs the transaction.

During the trial, core app features remain available; some Guides are purchase-gated. After purchase, Settings may unlock changing app language and light/dark appearance. Those preferences still stay on your device.

## 7. App language and localization

You choose a display language (for example English, Spanish, or Simplified Chinese) for menus, guides, and in-app help. That choice is stored on your device and is not transmitted to us. Official court PDFs and bundled federal forms remain in English regardless of UI language.

**Translations are not certified** and may be inaccurate. English rules and forms control. You are solely responsible for verifying information before you file. See our [Terms of Use](terms-of-use.md) for limitation of liability and user responsibilities.

## 8. Backups, share, and save to device

If you use the in-app backup feature, Forseti writes an archive **to a location you choose** on your device or cloud storage you control (via the system file picker). Print, Share, and Save-to-device actions for drafts likewise use Android system sheets or the Storage Access Framework under **your** control. We do not receive backup files or shared copies.

## 9. Children

Forseti is intended for adult self-represented litigants and is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country).
