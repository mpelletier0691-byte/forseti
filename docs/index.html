What Forseti does

  • Quick Jump — Federal Rules of Civil Procedure with search and a fast PDF reader (English PDFs).
  • Motion Drafts — Pro se forms and generated PDFs you can print or save.
  • Guides — Plain-language walkthroughs in your app language (English, Spanish, or Simplified Chinese).
  • References — Curated links including Federal Judicial Center materials and multilingual resources.
  • State Rules — Links to official rules; keep copies offline.
  • Deadlines — Rule 6–aware tracker with reminders and calendar export.
  • Case Profile — Per-case folders, PDF/Word viewing, and file ingestion.
  • Document Scanner — On-device OCR into your drafts.
  • Glossary & notes — Localized glossary, bookmarks, and annotations.

Languages

At first launch you choose one app language — English, Español, or 简体中文 — for menus, guides, glossary, and on-screen help. That choice stays on your device. Official court PDFs and bundled federal forms remain in English and are authoritative. Translations are uncertified and may be wrong — you must verify against official English sources.

Privacy & trust

No accounts. No ads. No telemetry. Your case files, notes, and deadlines stay on your device — we don’t run servers that receive your data.
