## 2. Brand foundation

### Philosophy

Asvaettir Labs builds tools for the determined: systems that restore clarity,
autonomy, and fairness in legal, technical, and digital spaces. The work blends
Norse craftsmanship, forensic discipline, and ethical AI principles.

### Core values

- **Offline-first privacy:** Sensitive work should remain under the user's
  control whenever possible.
- **Traceable reasoning:** Outputs should connect back to sources, documents,
  timestamps, or user-selected evidence.
- **Craftsmanship:** Interfaces should feel forged, disciplined, and precise,
  not decorative or cartoonish.
- **Human-centered judgment:** Tools support investigation and organization.
  They do not replace user responsibility, legal counsel, or professional
  review.

### Voice

Use language that is direct, serious, and grounded.

Preferred tone:

- Analytical
- Mythic, but restrained
- Practical
- Privacy-conscious
- Professional

Avoid:

- Overpromising legal outcomes
- Claims that AI replaces attorneys
- Cartoon Viking language
- Vague "revolutionary AI" phrasing
- Publishing confidential audit details on public pages

- **Headings:** Cinzel, Trajan-style, or another restrained Norse-inspired
  serif if available in Wix.
- **Body:** Inter, system sans, Segoe UI, or similar clean sans-serif.
- **Data labels:** JetBrains Mono, IBM Plex Mono, or Wix's closest monospace
  option.

If Wix font choices are limited, use a clean sans-serif for body text and
reserve the mythic look for logo imagery, icons, borders, and section labels.

- Forseti: raven, scales, rulebook, court columns.

- MuninnShade: raven, eye, evidence map, timeline nodes.
- VoltHalla: lightning, meter, waveform, utility grid.

Keep icons flat, high contrast, and simple enough to read on mobile.

## 4. Reusable Wix sections

### Product card

Use one card format for all tools.

**Fields:**

- Product logo or icon
- Product name
- One-line tagline
- 2-3 sentence description
- Status label
- Action button
- Optional screenshot carousel

**Card styling:**

- Charcoal panel
- 1 px rune-gold border at 25-35% opacity
- Small gold product label
- Dark image frame
- Button in gold or lightning blue depending on product

### Three-panel product layout

For product detail pages or expanded sections:

| Panel | Purpose | Wix content |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Left | Control actions | Import, configure, select, forge, analyze |
| Center | Workspace | Screenshot, diagram, timeline, graph, folder tree |
| Right | Output | Summary, warnings, report preview, recommendations |

On mobile, stack the panels in this order:

1. Product summary
2. Center visual
3. Actions
4. Output

**Headline:** Privacy and control by design.

**Items:**

- Offline-first workflows where possible
- No unnecessary telemetry
- User-controlled documents and exports
- Clear disclaimers and source-aware outputs
- Built for review, not blind reliance

### Brokkr Forge

**Headline:** Brokkr Forge

**Tagline:** The forge that shapes chaos into order.

**Description:** Brokkr Forge is a desktop organizer for transforming raw,
scattered folders into structured case directories. It is designed for repeatable
file organization, cleaner case intake, and a clearer record of where documents
were moved.

**Workflow:**

1. Select Folder - choose the source directory.
2. Choose Output - set the destination workspace.
3. Forge Structure - generate the organized hierarchy.
4. Log Chain of Custody - record file movements for later review.

**Mini-gallery captions:**

### VoltHalla

**Headline:** VoltHalla

**Tagline:** Where your watts meet their judgment.

**Description:** VoltHalla is an energy-forensics engine for analyzing utility

## 9. Accessibility checklist

Before publishing:

- Use high contrast text on every dark panel.
- Add alt text to every screenshot and logo.
- Make buttons readable without relying on glow effects.
- Keep headings in logical order: H1, H2, H3.
- Ensure mobile section stacking is clear.
- Avoid tiny gold text on black for body copy.
- Provide text alternatives for diagrams.
- Confirm contact form labels are visible and keyboard accessible.

## 10. Legal and trust language

### Suite disclaimer

Asvaettir Labs tools are built to support organization, analysis, and review.
They do not replace professional judgment, legal counsel, licensed technical
inspection, or user responsibility. Legal tools provide information and workflow
support only; they are not legal advice.

### Privacy statement

The Asvaettir Labs design philosophy favors offline-first workflows, local
control, and minimal exposure of sensitive data. Product-specific privacy
details should be reviewed in each tool's Privacy Policy before use.

### Confidentiality warning

Do not publish confidential technical audit reports, private case materials,
attorney work product, credentials, customer data, or unreleased internal
implementation details on the Wix site. Convert those materials into public,
high-level descriptions before posting.
