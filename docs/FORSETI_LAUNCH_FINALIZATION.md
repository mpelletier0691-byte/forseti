# Forseti Launch Finalization

This document captures Forseti's finalized launch positioning, trust
differentiators, pricing model, and internal growth-planning assumptions. Use it
to guide Wix copy, Play Store copy, launch planning, and founder-facing revenue
models.

## 1. Public product position

### Core positioning

Forseti is an offline, multilingual, mobile-friendly litigation-support toolkit
for pro se litigants in the United States.

### Public-safe headline

Forseti is your offline civil-procedure companion, built for people navigating
court systems without representation.

### Expanded public description

Forseti helps self-represented litigants organize federal civil procedure,
deadlines, official forms, notes, bookmarks, guides, and case documents in one
privacy-forward Android app. It is designed for clarity, independence, and
practical use under pressure.

Forseti is information only, not legal advice. It does not replace a licensed
attorney, court rules, local rules, standing orders, or the user's own
responsibility for filings and deadlines.

## 2. Finalized trust model

Forseti's trust position should be repeated consistently across the Wix site,
Play Store listing, screenshots, onboarding, and support materials:

- **One-time unlock:** $4.99 permanent unlock after the trial.
- **No subscription:** No recurring billing model.
- **Ad-free:** No advertising inside the app.
- **Offline-first:** Core content and workflows are built for use without
  constant connectivity.
- **No unnecessary data collection:** Legal users should not feel exploited,
  tracked, or profiled.
- **Multilingual support:** English, Spanish, and Simplified Chinese app
  language support for menus, guides, glossary, and help text.
- **Official federal materials:** Federal Rules of Civil Procedure and bundled
  federal forms are included from official public sources.
- **User-selectable PDFs:** Users can add or select trusted PDFs for their own
  reference workflows.
- **Procedure-focused guidance:** Guides help users understand process,
  deadlines, organization, and common mistakes without giving legal advice.
- **Digital organization:** Case folders, notes, bookmarks, deadlines, scans,
  and exports support practical e-filing preparation.

## 3. Market differentiators

Use these points in "Why Forseti" sections, Play Store feature bullets, Wix
product cards, and launch posts:

1. **One-time purchase builds trust.** Legal users are cautious about recurring
   fees. A permanent unlock is simple and predictable.
2. **No ads improves credibility.** Legal tools should feel professional and
   focused, not interrupted by monetized distractions.
3. **Privacy is central.** Self-represented litigants may be dealing with
   sensitive custody, housing, discrimination, employment, debt, or civil-rights
   issues.
4. **Offline access supports real-world use.** Court preparation should not
   depend on a perfect signal.
5. **Rules, templates, and guides create a workflow.** Forseti combines
   reference material, official forms, procedural guides, deadlines, and case
   organization instead of solving only one small problem.
6. **Multilingual access expands usefulness.** English, Spanish, and Simplified
   Chinese support helps more users understand the app interface and guides,
   while official court PDFs and federal forms remain authoritative in English.

## 5. Internal growth-planning assumptions

The numbers below are internal planning assumptions, not guarantees and not
public marketing claims. Do not place these projections on the Wix site, Play
Store listing, or public launch copy unless they are clearly framed as internal
scenario planning.

Known starting context:

- 17 installs
- 0 purchases while still in pre-launch / publishing stages
- $4.99 permanent unlock
- 3-day trial
- Quarterly updates planned
- Organic marketing as the initial channel

| Metric | Internal planning range |
| --- | --- |
| Installs | 3,500-8,000 |
| Paid conversion | 14-22% |
| Purchases | 490-1,760 |
| Gross revenue | $2,445-$8,782 |

### 6-month scenario

Assumptions: quarterly updates, PDF library expansion, more templates, more
guides, social media presence, and organic growth.

| Metric | Internal planning range |
| --- | --- |
| Installs | 20,000-45,000 |
| Purchases | 3,000-8,500 |
| Gross revenue | $14,970-$42,415 |

### 12-month scenario

Assumptions: multilingual support, official PDFs, improved templates, better
Play Store ranking, word of mouth, legal-aid visibility, and referral traffic.

| Metric | Internal planning range |
| --- | --- |
| Installs | 75,000-140,000 |
| Purchases | 11,000-28,000 |

If Forseti adds broader state rule sets, more languages, more templates, more
guides, stronger screenshots, legal-education content, and referral channels,
the internal upside target can include 250,000+ installs. Treat this as an
ambitious scenario, not a forecast.

## 6. Growth curve narrative

Use this for internal planning decks and launch strategy documents:

- **Months 1-3:** Slow but steady organic growth as the Play Store listing,
  screenshots, and trust message begin converting early users.
- **Months 3-6:** More reviews, updates, templates, and guide content can
  improve Play Store visibility and conversion.
- **Months 6-12:** Word of mouth, legal-aid visibility, multilingual access,
  and educational content can expand reach.
- **Year 2:** Additional state materials, languages, templates, guides, and
  referral partnerships can broaden the market.

## 7. Launch finalization checklist

Before public launch, verify:

- Google Play listing clearly states the $4.99 one-time unlock.
- Screenshots show no ads and no subscription prompts.
- Privacy policy matches actual data behavior.
- Data Safety answers match the implementation.
- Terms of Use state information-only / not legal advice.
- Store description avoids legal-outcome promises.
- Screenshots include FRCP, forms, guides, deadlines, scanner, case profile,
  glossary, notes, bookmarks, and multilingual support where appropriate.
- Trial and unlock copy is consistent across app, Play Store, and Wix.
- Accessibility screenshots and text are readable on small phones.
- Public copy does not publish internal revenue projections.
